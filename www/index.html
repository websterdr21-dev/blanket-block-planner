Taking a good photo

  • Lay the block flat and hold the phone straight above it.
  • Put it on something plain, a different colour from the block.
  • Get the whole block in, with a little space around the edges.
  • Use daylight where you can, and keep your shadow off it.

A loose tail of yarn is fine — it knows to ignore those.
